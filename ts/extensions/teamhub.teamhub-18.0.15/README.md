### **Don't let merge conflicts dictate your workflow.**

#### **Are branching strategies designed to avoid conflicts slowing you and your team down?**

If you are **assigning every developer a separate feature to build** be prepared for knowledge silos and individual features shipping no faster than they would with a single developer.

Whilst **doing away with branching all together** comes at the cost of additional development and testing to ensure work in progress doesn't break existing functionality.
